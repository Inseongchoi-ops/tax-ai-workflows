# FBAR Filing Checklist

## Client Information
- Full legal name
- SSN or ITIN
- Foreign address history
- Residency status

## Foreign Accounts Review
- Bank accounts
- Brokerage accounts
- Retirement accounts
- Crypto exchange accounts

## Threshold Review
- Aggregate foreign account value exceeded $10,000 at any point during the year

## Required Documentation
- Year-end statements
- Maximum balance information
- Account ownership details

## Filing Notes
- FBAR filed separately from tax return
- Due date follows individual tax return due date rules
- Retain records for at least 5 years
